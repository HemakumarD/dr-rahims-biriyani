The Biryani Story

A Royal Legacy
on Every Plate

Biriyani traces its roots to the royal kitchens of Persia, travelled through India, and found its heart in Hyderabad, where Mughal and Deccani flavours came together to create a legendary dish.

Rahim's Biriyani Co. carries that legacy forward — prepared fresh, sealed with care, and delivered to your table with the same devotion that has defined Hyderabadi cooking for generations.

Origin
Persian Royal Kitchens
Spiced rice and slow-cooked meat — the foundations of an enduring culinary tradition.
Arrival
The Indian Subcontinent
Traders and courtiers carry the tradition across the subcontinent, absorbing local spices along the way.
Refinement
The Deccan Courts
Saffron, dum cooking and local ingredients transform biriyani into something entirely new.
Home
Hyderabad — The Final Destination
Mughal technique meets Deccani flavour. The legendary Hyderabadi Biriyani is born and perfected.
The Complete Meal

Everything in One Pack

A complete Hyderabadi feast, sealed fresh in our signature three-compartment pack — ready to serve.

1
Chicken Biriyani
675 g · Main Compartment

Slow-cooked Hyderabadi-style chicken biriyani with long-grain basmati, whole spices, saffron and caramelised onions. Prepared fresh — no preservatives, no shortcuts.

2
Onion Raitha
120 mL · Side 1

Chilled, creamy yoghurt folded with thinly sliced red onion and fresh coriander, finished with a dusting of roasted cumin. Cool and refreshing — the perfect balance to every spicy mouthful of biriyani.

3
Mirchi ka Salan
120 mL · Side 2

Hyderabad's classic partner to biriyani — long green chillies slow-cooked in a rich, nutty gravy of roasted peanuts, sesame and coconut, brightened with tangy tamarind. Gently spiced, deeply flavourful, and made to be spooned over every bite.
